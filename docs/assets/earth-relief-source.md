# Earth Relief

Natural Earth Gray Earth, 1:50 million, shaded relief, hypsography and ocean bottom.
Version 3.2.0. Public-domain cartographic data, not real-time satellite imagery.

Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-raster-data/50m-gray-earth/
Archive: https://naturalearth.s3.amazonaws.com/50m_raster/GRAY_50M_SR_OB.zip
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The 10800 x 5400 global Plate Carree TIFF was converted to WebP by
`scripts/prepare_relief.py`. Pixel colors are rendered at 16% opacity over the
vector basemap. National boundaries use the existing Natural Earth 110m asset.
