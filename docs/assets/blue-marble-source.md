# Satellite Background

NASA Blue Marble Next Generation, July 2004, global 5400 x 2700 JPEG,
with topography and bathymetry shading. This is a satellite-derived monthly
composite, not live imagery; ocean-floor relief is bathymetric shading, not
an optical photograph of the ocean floor. The same background serves both
catchment seasons and is not an analytical input.

Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/
Image: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography-bathymetry/july/world.topo.bathy.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg
Credit: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation.

The original JPEG is displayed at 38% opacity over white. No land polygons,
coastline strokes, graticules or Antarctic boundary lines are drawn.
The only line overlay is Natural Earth land administrative boundaries.
